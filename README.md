## How I approach quality

Quality is a whole-team responsibility. Testing helps us discover what we do not yet understand about the product, its risks and the people using it. Agents can extend that work; the team still owns the decisions.

I bring exploratory testing, London and Chicago TDD, XP practices, and risk-based coverage into this work. PACTS — **Proactive, Autonomous, Collaborative, Targeted, Structured** — gives me a way to discuss agent behavior without losing the context that makes a test useful.

When a tool generates tests, I want to know what they exercise, whether their assertions can catch a defect, and what we should investigate next. You can inspect that approach in [AQE's quality gates](https://github.com/proffesor-for-testing/agentic-qe/blob/main/docs/loki-mode-features.md) and [skill validation guide](https://github.com/proffesor-for-testing/agentic-qe/blob/main/docs/guides/skill-validation.md). The [interaction benchmark](https://github.com/proffesor-for-testing/agentic-qe/blob/main/benchmarks/interaction/results/README.md) also records a small experiment with no demonstrated benefit. Evidence includes limits and corrections.
