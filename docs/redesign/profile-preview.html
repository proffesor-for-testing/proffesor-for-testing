How I approach quality

Quality is a whole-team responsibility. Testing helps us discover what we do not yet understand about the product, its risks and the people using it. Agents can extend that work; the team still owns the decisions.

I bring exploratory testing, London and Chicago TDD, XP practices, and risk-based coverage into this work. PACTS — Proactive, Autonomous, Collaborative, Targeted, Structured — gives me a way to discuss agent behavior without losing the context that makes a test useful.

When a tool generates tests, I want to know what they exercise, whether their assertions can catch a defect, and what we should investigate next. You can inspect that approach in AQE's quality gates and skill validation guide. The interaction benchmark also records a small experiment with no demonstrated benefit. Evidence includes limits and corrections.

Learning and community

I speak at testing conferences, write about the move from classical to agentic QE, and help build the Serbian Agentics Foundation Chapter. My earlier work includes growing Alchemy's QA/QE organization and serving as Belgrade Test Conference Program Chair (2017–2019).
